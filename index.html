Perspective

Isometric

Parallel lines never meet, so sizes stay the same at any depth. Pixel art 2:1 steps two pixels across for every one up, which keeps tile edges clean in games.

Vanishing points

Drag the points and the horizon on the board. Points can sit off the page, which gives a calmer, less distorted grid.

Objects

A figure as tall as the viewer has its eyes on the horizon wherever it stands, which sets the scale of the scene. Drag its head to make it taller or shorter.

Drag an object to move it, and its square handles to change its size. Objects follow the vanishing points when you move them. Delete removes the selected one.

Objects work in 1, 2 and 3-point and isometric grids.

Reference image

Put a photo or sketch under the grid, then line the vanishing points up with its edges. You can also drop an image onto the board.

Guides

Isometric grids have no horizon or vanishing points.

The cone shows where the grid stays undistorted. Keep your subject inside it, or push the points further apart.

Page

Drag points or the horizon. Drag empty space to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
